Release

One bottling, then the casks retire.

These casks have aged in our acetaia for decades, losing a little to evaporation every summer. What is left is dark, dense, and ready. This release is the final draw from that row of wood, and we have no plans to repeat it.

First

Fire

Trebbiano and Lambrusco grapes are pressed whole, and the must is cooked slowly over open flame until it darkens and concentrates into something closer to syrup than juice.

Then

Wood

The cooked must moves through a descending line of casks: oak, chestnut, cherry, ash and mulberry. Each wood absorbs a share and flavors what remains.

Finally

Draw

When the master taster judges the smallest cask ready, a single draw is taken. That draw is this bottle. What remains stays in the wood for the next generation.

Our acetaia has worked this row of barrels since Tuscanini began. This bottling retires it.

Serving

For the last minute of the meal.

A few drops over aged parmesan, split figs, or vanilla gelato is all it takes.
